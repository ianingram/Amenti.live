**For whoever edits `Page1.html` next · Ingram Manor LLC · 29 August 2026**

`Page1.html` is 525 KB and holds six sections, thirty-six named controls and
four separate conversation surfaces. This is the part of it a walk cannot
derive: what each section is FOR, how they reach each other, and which
controls are load-bearing.

## 0 · THE THREE THINGS THAT COST A SESSION

**THE BUNDLE.** `amenti-chat.js`, `amenti-voice.js`, `amenti-listen.js`,
`amenti-resolve.js` and `config.js` are compiled into
**`amenti-core.bundle.js`**, and the page loads the bundle. Editing a source
and pushing it **does nothing**. On 28 Aug three conversation modes were
written, tested by 141 assertions and pushed — and were inert, because the
bundle was not rebuilt.

**THE WILDCARD RESET.** `amenti-tabs.css` line 279 carries

```css
body[data-page="terminal"] *{margin:0;padding:0;box-sizing:border-box;}
```

Two selectors, so it **outranks any single class** no matter where in the file
that class appears. A padding or margin set on anything inside the terminal
needs `!important` or it silently becomes zero. This cost four attempts at one
gutter before a probe read the computed value and found 0px where the file
said 44.

**DUPLICATE SELECTORS AND DUPLICATE IDS.** Three separate rules were declared
twice in one night — `.term-mode-wrap`, `.term-ctx`, `.term-call` — and in each
case the stale copy was later in the file and therefore the one that ran. The
markup has the same fault: **`quiz-close` is declared twice**, so
`getElementById` returns the first and the second binding is unreachable. Run
`csstest.js` before handing anything over.

> **A DUPLICATE IS NOT A STYLE PROBLEM. IT IS A FILE WHERE THE THING YOU ARE
> READING IS NOT THE THING THAT RUNS.**

## 1 · THE SIX SECTIONS

Sections are switched by `data-page` on `<body>`; each is a
`<section class="page-section" data-page="…">`. **There are six, and they are
not the eleven tabs in the nav:**

```
   arena · bookstore · codex · counsel · terminal · timeline
```

**BROWSE, ASK AMENTI, INTERFACE, MARKETPLACE, GAMEROOM and COURT are not
sections of this file.** They are links out — to `hall.html`, `Page2.html`,
`court.html`, the gameroom's own repo. A nav label is not a section, and the
register's `where.section` field uses the READER'S name for a place, which is
why it lists `browse` and `dispatch` that `data-page` does not.

`timeline` has no entry in the surface register at all.

| section | is for | named controls | spends |
|---|---|---|---|
| **arena** | the front. A stranger's first thirty seconds. | 4 | mint |
| **codex** | one figure in full, to decide who to talk to | 2 | free |

| **counsel** | three figures on one question | 6 | anthropic · gemini |
| **bookstore** | the chapters | — | free |
| **timeline** | **undescribed — in no register** | ? | ? |

Plus the quiz, the media player and the dispatch reader, which are **overlays**

**The arena and the codex both END at the terminal.** `hero-cta-quiz` opens

reader into the terminal already tuned. **The codex is a chooser, not a
destination** — treat any change that makes it terminal-like as a mistake.

## 2 · THE FOUR CONVERSATION SURFACES, AND WHY THEY ARE FOUR

This is the single most important thing in the file and the least obvious.
**Four places hold a conversation and they do not share a prompt.**

```
   terminal-input  → Amenti.chat            (the bundle · five modes)
   counsel-input   → Amenti.chat            (the bundle · same engine)
   cnsGo           → counselSys()           ← ITS OWN PROMPT, in Page1.html
   hall-ask        → AmentiHall.ask         (hall.html · a different page)
```

**`counselSys()` is a second counsel prompt that the chat core knows nothing
about.** Change the counsel register in `amenti-chat.js` and the chamber does
not move. This is the standing architectural question: two prompts exist, and
adding modes to the chamber would make it worse rather than better. **Do not
"fix" it by copying the register — that is how the register ended up in two
files before.**

**`cnsGo` is the most expensive press in the product.** One CONVENE is **four
model calls** — three figures in parallel, then a synthesis — and every one is
unique text, so **none of it caches**. It is also not seen by the visit
reading.

## 4 · THE TERMINAL, IN DETAIL

Three columns in one grid, `.term-shell`.

```
   .term-roster    260px   FREQUENCIES — a thousand names, scrolls
   .term-main      1fr     the plate, the stream, the input rail
   .term-ctx       280px   CONNECTION · DOSSIER · ABILITIES · HINTS · MODE
```

**The shell has a fixed height and its children carry `min-height:0`.** Both
are required. A grid child's default `min-height` is `auto`, meaning it refuses
to shrink below its content — so without them a thousand-name roster grows the
row to thousands of pixels, stretching the plate and pushing the input rail off
the screen. `#charList`'s own `overflow-y:auto` cannot engage in a column with
no ceiling.

**The context panel does not scroll; its upper half does.** `.term-ctx` is a
flex column holding `.term-ctx-scroll` (takes the slack) and `.term-mode-wrap`
(the fixed foot). `margin-top:auto` was tried three times and cannot work here:
it pushes into free space, and a box with `overflow-y:auto` sizes to its
content and therefore has none.

**The MODE control is the one a reader will miss.** It decides what the figure
is FOR and it holds across every turn — five values, a closed set guarded in
`setMode`, each with a register block in the bundle. It has been moved four
times: toolbar (read as an action), roster foot (the list scrolls through that
space), input rail (in among the buttons), and finally the panel foot, where it
reads as a fourth section beside CONNECTION and DOSSIER.

**The rail's order is now declared, not inherited.** Speaker, mic, CALL and
stop are each injected by a separate script with `insertBefore(hint)`, so they
landed in whatever order their scripts ran. CALL is placed explicitly after
`.term-input`.

## 5 · WHAT REACHES WHAT

```
   amenti-core.bundle.js  → terminal, counsel, the reading rooms
   amenti-dial.js         → the CALL sequence. Depends on NOTHING in the bundle.
   amenti-memory.js       → the only file that knows the memory token and URL
   amenti-qr.js           → ?via= arrival tracking, sessionStorage
   amenti-visits.js       → the visit reading — NOT WIRED INTO Page1
   names.csv              → the roster. Page1 and amenti-voice.js fetch it
                            SEPARATELY. Two loads of one file.
```

**`amenti-visits.js` is not in the page.** `AmentiVisits.begin` and
`AmentiVisits.end` appear nowhere in `Page1.html`. The visit reading was built
and never mounted.

**`window.claude.acceptsSystemTail` is not set**, so prompt caching does not
engage. The builders return `{ head, tail }` and the saving — about 33% a visit
— is available and unclaimed.

## 6 · THE OVERLAYS

**The quiz** (`AmentiQuiz`) — mints against the emerald ledger. `quiz-listen`
speaks in the RECITAL register, so it hits the R2 archive and is nearly free;
`cnsGo` on the same page caches nothing. **The two economies sit one section
apart.**

**The media player** (`AmentiAudio`) — transport for ALREADY-RENDERED audio.
Its four controls cost nothing; the bill was paid when the clip was made.
`mp-close` must STOP playback, not merely hide the bar.

**The dispatch reader** — already rendered when it opens.

## 7 · THE STANDING ORDERS

**Rebuild the bundle.** Every time. The page does not load the loose files.

**Run `csstest.js` before handing over.** It strips comments and media queries
and fails on any `.term-` selector declared twice. It exists because three were.

**Prove it on glass.** A passing harness is not the last step. Fourteen
harnesses reported green while the Terminal ran an inline fallback, and a test
that creates its own object tests the object, not the system.

**When a screenshot says nothing changed, probe — do not edit again.**
`probes/probe-ctx-gutter.js` reads the computed geometry and names which rule
produced it. It found the wildcard reset in one run, after four edits had not.

**Page1.html is NEVER edited to add a figure.** See `PIPELINE.md`.
