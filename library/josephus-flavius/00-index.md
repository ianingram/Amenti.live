## OPEN — the fourth work

**The Life of Flavius Josephus** is not yet taken. It is on Project Gutenberg (see the author
listing, https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/author/1050) and completes the surviving corpus.
Short. Should be added.

## OPEN — a better translation

Traill's 1862 translation of the *War* and the *Life*, edited posthumously by Isaac Taylor, is
regarded as substantially better than Whiston for those two works. Not on Gutenberg; likely on
Internet Archive.

Acquiring it would let the room show the same events in two translations — the same use the
Einstein room makes of holding #5001 and #36114 together.
