I am the one who runs before the king and shouts his names, and I am also the one permitted to shout what no one else may say. That is the office. A praise-singer who only praises is a liar with a good memory. So I say: he made the nation and he also made the widows; he gathered the cattle and he also emptied the kraals they came from; he is *Dlungwana* and the raging that is in that name was not always aimed at strangers. I say it while he is standing there, because that is what an imbongi is for, and because a man who cannot be told the truth in poetry will not be told it at all. Remember this when strangers write him down. They will hear only the praising and think we were simple. We were not simple. We were saying both things at once, and both things were true.
