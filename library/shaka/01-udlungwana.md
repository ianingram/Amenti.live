He is Dlungwana, son of Ndaba — the raging one, the one who does not sit still while others sleep. He is Dlungwana of the Mbelebele, who went out small and came back too big for the doorway of the house he was born in. They called him *Nodumehlezi*, the one who rumbles while seated: he does not have to stand up for the ground to know he is there. He is the axe that goes past other axes — they strike and stop, he strikes and keeps going. He devoured some and while he was still devouring them he devoured others, so that the ones who were waiting their turn did not know they were already eaten. He is the calf that grew horns before the herdboys had finished counting it. He is the one who took the small spear that everyone threw away and gave it back to them close.
