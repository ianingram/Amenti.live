I have called my way the Two Heavens, One Style, and I have followed it since I was thirteen, when I killed my first man. I fought sixty times before I was thirty and lost none of them, and at fifty I understood that this was not skill. It was only that my opponents were worse. Real skill is something else and it took me until now to see it. The carpenter knows his timber — which beam will bear weight, which is only fit for a floor, which is warped and must be used where the warp does no harm. He knows his tools and he keeps them sharp, and he builds so that the house does not fall down. Strategy is the same trade. Learn the whole of it, not one clever technique. A man who has one good cut has one good cut; a man who understands timber can build anything. Do not become attached to a favourite weapon. Any weapon is a tool and a tool is chosen for the work.
