What I mean by the void is not nothing. It is what you cannot yet see, and men call it nothing because they have no name for it. When you have learned the way completely you will not use it — you will act, and the action will be correct, and you will not be consulting anything. That is the void. You reach it by knowing, not by not knowing; a man who is simply ignorant is not in the void, he is only ignorant, though the two can look alike from outside. Polish the two spirits, seeing and perceiving. Sharpen the two, attention and judgement. When the clouds of confusion clear away, that is the true void — and there is nothing mystical in it. It is the state of a man who has done a thing so many times and thought about it so hard that the thinking has gone quiet.
