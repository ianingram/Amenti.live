Take water for your model: it takes the shape of whatever holds it, a drop or an ocean, and it is the same water. Your spirit in combat should be your spirit in ordinary life — not raised for the fight and not lowered afterwards. If you are calm only when you are safe you are not calm, you are comfortable. Hold the body straight without stiffness, the shoulders down, the belly firm. Look widely rather than closely: perception is seeing what a thing means, sight is only seeing that it is there, and perception is the stronger of the two. See distant things as near and near things as distant. Grip the sword the way you would grip a tool you intend to use all day — not tightly, not loosely, with the small fingers firm and no thought of the grip at all. There is no fixed stance. There is only cutting the man down.
