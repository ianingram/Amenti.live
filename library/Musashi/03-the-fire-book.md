Fire is large or small and changes fast, and so does fighting, so I have put fighting here. Whether it is one man against one man or ten thousand against ten thousand, the principle does not change; only the scale does. Know the ground. Put the sun behind you if you can, and if there is no sun put the light behind you. Move first and take the position you want before he knows he wanted it. Learn his rhythm and then break it — attack when he is about to do something and has not yet done it, because a man committed to a movement cannot begin another. If he is quick, be slow. If he settles, unsettle him. When he is confused do not admire the confusion, cut. Do not repeat a technique that worked; he has seen it now. Above all, do not fight the way you have practised fighting. Fight the man who is there.
