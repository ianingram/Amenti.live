Monday, 10th of September In this day and night he made 60 leagues, at the rate of 10 miles an hour, which are 2 and a half leagues; but he only counted 48 leagues, that the people might not be alarmed if the voyage should be long. Tuesday, 11th of September That day they sailed on their course, which was west, and made 20 leagues and more. They saw a large piece of the mast of a ship of 120 tons, but were unable to get it.
