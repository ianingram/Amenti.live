## HOW TO CHECK A ROW

**`ATTICA.csv`** — every row has a `uri`. Open it. Pleiades will show you the
same coordinate, the same precision, and its own bibliography.

**`GEO.json`** — the gazetteer is hash-pinned. `gazetteer.sha256` is the exact
`cities15000` the pins were built from, and `matchesAudited` says whether the
join still holds.

**`NARROWS.csv`** — you cannot check a row against a source, because the
coordinates were typed from knowledge and no gazetteer of passes exists. That
is why `NARROWS-terrain.csv` exists: it reports what the earth says under each
one, and names the three that disagree.

**`SEATS.csv`, `SITES.csv`, `EVENTS.csv`** — authored. The `note` column carries
what is known; there is no per-row citation yet, and **that is the gap #75 names
as the one to close while a register is being written rather than after.**

## WHAT IS GENERATED AND MUST NOT BE EDITED

    ROSTER-INDEX.json · GEO.json · EVENTS.json · GRAPH.json · img/KEYS.json
    NARROWS-terrain.csv · ATTICA*.csv · GROUND.jpg · ATTICA.jpg

Each names its generator in its own header. Editing one is a change that the
next run silently reverses, which is worse than a change that fails.
