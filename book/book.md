# KEYS TO THE BLOOD
### The chronicle of Amenti — a true account, written in the field

*Ingram Manor LLC · Under the command of Captain Ian Ingram*

---

> **What this book is.** A contemporaneous chronicle of the building of Amenti —
> the library of the dead, the hall that lets them answer, and the machine that
> taught them to hear one another. Each chapter was written during the days it
> describes and has not been smoothed afterward. Where a later account disagrees
> with a chapter written in the field, **the field account wins.** The book keeps
> its wrong turns as evidence; a campaign remembered only by its victories is a
> worse record than one that keeps its failures.

*The numbering is sparse by design: these are the milestone chapters, each a
self-contained campaign captured while it ran. The gaps between them are days
that were lived but not chronicled — the record keeps only the entries of record.*

*The hall is a doorway; the souls are the destination; and the lines between them
are drawn only where a source will bear the weight.*
