Six prompts: card face + reading room for three figures. Reading rooms follow the
Lincoln-at-the-column pattern — reposed, informal, absorbed, raking side light, the
figure occupied rather than presenting.

LIKENESS — Douglass and Tesla are among the most photographed people of their
centuries, so a real likeness is grounded. Caesar has no photograph but the Tusculum
bust is generally held to be a portrait taken in his lifetime; the prompt names it so
the model works from the surviving evidence rather than from the idealised imperial
type invented after his death.
