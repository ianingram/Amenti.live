### ALBERT EINSTEIN — CARD
status:  SHIPPED — the age guard held

status:  AWAITING GENERATION — 9:16, 2K, polish ON. key: viewer's right

Albert Einstein outside the Swiss Federal Office for Intellectual Property at night, Bern, 1905. Twenty-six years old, technical expert third class. THICK DARK BROWN HAIR neatly cut and combed, a full dark moustache, a young unlined face, dark alert eyes. Wearing a plain dark three-piece wool suit, slightly worn, a high starched white collar and dark necktie, a watch chain across the waistcoat, a dark overcoat open over it. Under one arm a bundle of PATENT FILES tied with tape; in the other hand a rolled sheaf of his own PAPER, notably scruffier. Standing on the wet arcade pavement of the Kramgasse under the stone arches, the office door shut behind him for the day. Wet cobbles, wet stone, a cast-iron street lamp, tram rails set in the road catching the light, the shuttered shopfronts under the arcade going away into dark. Cold Alpine night, drizzle just stopped, everything throwing hard highlights. KEY LIGHT FROM THE VIEWER'S RIGHT: a single gas street lamp under the arcade, hard and low, raking across the wet stone and one side of his face; the depth of the arcade and the shadow side fall to true black. camera at chest height from a few paces along the arcade. FOUR GUARDS, AND THE FIRST IS THE WHOLE PLATE. ONE, HE IS TWENTY-SIX, NOT SEVENTY-SIX. THICK DARK BROWN HAIR, neatly cut and combed, a full dark moustache, a young unlined face, dark alert eyes. NOT the white-haloed old man of the famous photographs — no wild hair, no white hair, no cardigan, no pipe, no tongue out. That likeness is fifty years later and it is the only Einstein the model knows; refuse it outright. TWO, PERIOD DRESS OF 1905 BERN: a plain dark three-piece wool suit, a high starched collar and a dark necktie, a watch chain. Neat, slightly worn, a clerk's suit and not a professor's. THREE, NO ICONOGRAPHY. NO blackboard, NO chalk, NO E=mc squared written anywhere, NO equations floating in the air, NO stars, NO galaxies, NO clocks bending, NO laboratory glassware. He worked with a pen, paper and a government desk. FOUR, NO MODERN ANYTHING — this is 1905: gas or early electric light, no plastic, no fluorescent tube, no screen of any kind. Hard highlights run along every fold and edge; deep true blacks in between. One dominant key, hard-edged, with a clear terminator across the face and body; the shadow side falls almost to black and dissolves into the ground, held only by a thin rim of bounce. Full tonal range with small blown speculars. Volume carried by continuous tonal turn. Fine film grain, deep vignette, high micro-contrast. Waist-up, cropped just below the belt. Albert Einstein at his desk in the patent office, late, Bern, 1905. He examined patent applications eight hours a day for a government salary and wrote the papers that broke physics in the hours around it — special relativity, the photoelectric effect, Brownian motion, and the mass-energy paper, all in one year, at this desk. Twenty-six, thick dark hair, dark moustache, in shirtsleeves with the waistcoat unbuttoned, collar loosened, the jacket over the chair behind him. Seated at a plain oak government desk, hunched close, WRITING FAST with a steel-nibbed pen. THE DESK IS DIVIDED AND THAT IS THE POINT. On one side the job: a stack of PATENT APPLICATIONS in grey card covers tied with tape, a technical drawing of a gear mechanism half unrolled and weighted flat, a rubber date stamp, an official ink pad, a ledger. On the other side the work: loose sheets of cheap paper covered edge to edge in a small dense hand and diagrams, more sheets on the floor by his shoe, an inkwell, a spare nib, a cold cup of coffee. One drawer is open with more of his own paper stuffed in it, out of sight of the door. Behind him the office: rows of pigeonholed wooden shelving stuffed with grey files going up into the dark, a wall clock, a coat stand, other empty desks in a row — everyone else has gone home. KEY LIGHT FROM THE VIEWER'S LEFT, low and close: a single green-shaded desk lamp, the only light burning in the room, falling off fast so the far shelving and the empty desks drop to true black. Cold room; his breath faintly visible. Polished brass, the wet ink and the glass of the lamp carry every highlight. camera at desk height from across the office, as if you had come back for something and found him there.
