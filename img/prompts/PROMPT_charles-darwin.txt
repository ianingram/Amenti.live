THE AGE GUARD IS THE WHOLE RISK, AND IT IS THE THIRD TIME THIS SESSION.
Einstein came back right at 26 and not 76. Dickens came back right at 31 and not 60.
Darwin is the same shape and the strongest case of the three: the white-bearded
patriarch is the ONLY Darwin anyone pictures, and that beard does not arrive until
the 1860s, after illness, thirty years after the Beagle. At twenty-six he is
CLEAN-SHAVEN with reddish-brown hair and side-whiskers.

Age is arithmetic, not preference: born 1809, Galapagos 1835, the sketch 1844.

THE CARD IS THE PASSAGE ITSELF. The stored Galapagos text is about how unafraid the
birds are — a mocking-thrush lands on the rim of a tortoise-shell pitcher he is
holding and drinks from it. That bird is in the frame, and so is a dead one on the
rock beside him, because he was collecting and observing in the same motion.

THE TERMINAL IS THE WAITING. In 1844 he enlarged the sketch to 230 pages, had it
fair-copied, and left instructions and money for his wife to publish it if he died.
Then he said nothing for fourteen years. So he is NOT writing: one hand flat on the
finished manuscript, the other holding a letter, and the barnacles he buried himself
in for eight years instead are on the desk beside him.

WHAT TO REJECT ON SIGHT. The beard. Any monkey, ape, skeleton or evolution diagram —
that iconography is other people's and it is a century of cartoons. A bright room.

### CHARLES DARWIN — TERMINAL

Charles Darwin aged 35 in 1844 at his desk at night, CLEAN-SHAVEN with reddish-brown hair receding at the temples and long side-whiskers, a drawn tired face. Dark coat over a waistcoat, seated in a worn leather chair, a board laid across the arms as a writing surface. He is NOT writing. One hand rests flat on a thick stack of manuscript pages tied with tape; the other holds a sealed letter. On the desk a brass microscope, barnacle specimens in glass dishes, a pencil, loose sheets covered in a cramped hand, a candle burned low. Behind him shelves of books and specimen boxes going up into darkness, a shuttered window, a cold fireplace. Single candle and one oil lamp from the right, falling off fast, the far wall true black.
