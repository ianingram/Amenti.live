### FLAVIUS JOSEPHUS — CARD
status:  SHIPPED

status:  AWAITING GENERATION — 9:16, 2K, polish ON. key: viewer's right

Josephus on the wall at Jotapata, the forty-seventh night of the siege. Galilee, AD 67. Thirty years old — a priest's son given command of a province he was not trained to defend. Levantine, olive-brown skin, dark hair, a full dark beard cut close, hollow with exhaustion, dust in every crease. Wearing a plain undyed wool tunic under a scale corselet that does not fit him well, a leather war belt, a dark mantle pulled over one shoulder against the cold, a short sword he is not touching. Dirt to the elbows. STANDING AT THE PARAPET, both hands flat on the stone, LOOKING DOWN AND OUT at what is coming. Below and beyond him in the dark: THE ROMAN SIEGE WORKS — a great earth ramp built up toward the wall, engines under hide screens, a palisaded camp laid out in exact rectangles running away into the night, and hundreds of small watch fires in ordered rows across the whole valley floor. The order of it is the point: it is not a mob, it is a machine, and it is nearly finished. Behind him on the wall, two or three of his own men sitting slumped against the stone, done in. KEY LIGHT FROM THE VIEWER'S RIGHT, low and hard: a pitch torch guttering in an iron bracket on the parapet, raking across the stone and one side of his face and the dulled scale of the corselet; everything beyond the torch falls to true black, and the valley below reads only as the pattern of fires. Cold night; breath visible; smoke and dust hanging. camera at chest height along the wall walk, slightly low. FOUR GUARDS, and they exist only to stop the picture reading WRONG — the name is printed on every surface of this product, so nothing here has to identify him. ONE, HE IS A FIRST-CENTURY JUDAEAN — Levantine, olive-brown skin, dark hair, a full dark beard cut close. NOT European, NOT fair, NOT a Roman senator type. TWO, THIS IS THE SECOND TEMPLE PERIOD, NOT LATER JUDAISM. NO black hat, NO shtreimel, NO long coat, NO modern Orthodox dress of any kind, NO Torah scroll with modern finials, NO Star of David — that symbol is centuries later. He wears a plain wool or linen tunic and a mantle. THREE, NO CRUCIFIXES, NO CHURCHES, NO CHRISTIAN ICONOGRAPHY — he is a Jewish priest writing for Romans and none of that exists in his world. FOUR, HE IS NOT A WARRIOR HERO. He is a young provincial commander who is losing, and later a middle-aged writer in a rich man's house. No raised sword, no heroic posture, either plate. Visible atmosphere catches the key so the light reads as a physical presence in the air and separates the figure from the background. Cinematic monochrome character plate, near black-and-white with a single warm accent carried only on metal. EVERY SURFACE IS WET, OILED OR POLISHED so it throws a hard specular highlight — nothing matte, nothing that eats the light. Hard highlights run along every fold and edge; deep true blacks in between. One dominant key, hard-edged, with a clear terminator across the face and body; the shadow side falls almost to black and dissolves into the ground, held only by a thin rim of bounce. Full tonal range with small blown speculars. Volume carried by continuous tonal turn. Fine film grain, deep vignette, high micro-contrast. Waist-up, cropped just below the belt. Subject centred, facing the viewer, shoulders filling the width of the frame. The head occupies the top third with a hand's width of space above it. Vertical format, aspect ratio 9:16. Background is a dark, shallow-focus suggestion of place, two stops below the figure. Both hands fully visible and clearly resolved — palm, finger mass, thumb, knuckles, tendons on the back of the hand. Headgear sits firmly ON the skull and casts a shadow onto the brow that binds it to the head. Face weathered and specific, skull structure reading through: brow ridge, cheekbone, jaw, temple. Skin has pores, scars and a faint sheen. Expression exhausted and calculating — a commander doing arithmetic he does not like the answer to. Not heroic, not defiant, not afraid. Working out how this ends. Photographic realism. Materials behave like real materials under a hard light. Sharp on the face, softer toward the frame edge.

### FLAVIUS JOSEPHUS — TERMINAL
status:  SHIPPED with two period errors: gothic windows and a bound codex

status:  AWAITING GENERATION — 9:16, 2K, polish ON. key: viewer's left

Josephus writing The Jewish War in Rome, in the house of the men who won it. About AD 75. Thirty-eight, softer than he was, the beard grey-flecked and neatly trimmed now, the hands clean. He has Roman citizenship, a pension, an apartment in the Flavian house, and a name that is not the one he was born with. Seated at a writing table in a quiet upper room, in a plain Roman tunic with a mantle over one shoulder — DRESSED AS A ROMAN. Writing steadily onto a papyrus roll with a reed pen, mid-line, the roll running off the table edge to the floor. THE TABLE IS THE WHOLE STORY AND IT IS DIVIDED. On one side, ROMAN MATERIAL: bound wooden tablets and official campaign commentarii in neat capsae, a folded itinerary, a plan of a city drawn in a hard engineer's hand — the besiegers' own records, given to him. On the other side, nothing but his own memory: loose sheets in a small crowded hand, corrections, one sheet crumpled and dropped. Behind him the room is Roman and comfortable and not his: a fresco panel, a bronze lamp stand, a couch, shuttered windows. It is a good room and he is a guest in it. KEY LIGHT FROM THE VIEWER'S LEFT, low and close: a single bronze oil lamp on the table, falling off fast so the fresco, the couch and the far wall drop to true black and only the papyrus, the pen, his hands and one side of his face carry light. Lamp smoke rising through the key. camera at table height from across the room, as if you had come in and he had not stopped writing.
