### CONFUCIUS — CARD
status:  SHIPPED

status:  AWAITING GENERATION — 9:16, 2K, polish ON. key: viewer's left

Confucius on the road, in the doorway of a provincial ruler's hall he has just been turned away from. The state of Wei, about 495 BC. Sixty-odd years old, tall and heavy-boned, big-handed, with a long grey beard and moustache and thinning grey hair in a simple topknot under a plain dark cloth cap. HIS SKIN IS DEEP TANNED REDDISH-BROWN, weathered and coarsened and deeply lined, the hands and forearms as dark as the face, the skin faintly oiled so it throws hard speculars — fourteen years of open cart in all weather are on him. Wearing a heavy plain robe of coarse undyed hemp in dark earth colours, wrapped LEFT OVER RIGHT and belted with a broad woven sash, a plain jade ornament hanging from it, heavy cloth shoes, road dust to the knee. One hand holding a BUNDLE OF BAMBOO WRITING SLIPS bound with cord against his side; the other loose at his side. Standing on the worn stone threshold, half turned to the viewer, about to walk away. Behind him a heavy timber gate and the packed-earth wall of the compound, a bronze ring handle, and beyond it the courtyard falling into shadow. Rain has just stopped: wet stone, wet timber, water standing in the ruts, everything throwing hard highlights. KEY LIGHT FROM THE VIEWER'S LEFT, low and hard — late sun breaking under cloud along the wall, raking across the wet stone and the tanned face and the coarse hemp, the shadow side falling to true black. camera at chest height a few paces out in the road. FOUR GUARDS, all default failures. ONE, HIS SKIN IS DEEP TANNED REDDISH-BROWN — sun-coarsened, weathered, deeply lined, the hands and forearms as dark as the face, the skin oiled and catching hard light. He spent fourteen years travelling between states in an open ox cart and he looks it. NOT pale, NOT smooth, NOT the fair silk-clad scholar of later Chinese court portraiture — those were painted a thousand years after he died by men who never went outdoors. TWO, HE IS OLD, TALL AND HEAVY-BONED — sixty-odd, well over six feet by the accounts, big hands, a long grey beard and moustache, thinning grey hair pulled into a simple topknot under a plain cloth cap. THREE, THIS IS THE SIXTH CENTURY BC, NOT LATER CHINA. Plain heavy hemp and coarse undyed silk in dark earth colours, wrapped left over right with a sash. NO Ming or Qing dress, NO dragon robes, NO mandarin squares, NO queue, NO fan, NO round scholar's spectacles, NO porcelain, NO paper — writing is on BAMBOO SLIPS bound with cord. FOUR, HE IS NOT A CONTENTED SAGE. He failed. He was turned away by every ruler he approached and went home at sixty-eight having convinced nobody. No beatific smile, no serenity, no gilded temple. Two or three more disciples walk behind, one leading the ox, one with a carrying pole. In the cart bed: bundles of BAMBOO WRITING SLIPS bound with cord, a rolled sleeping mat, a water gourd, a cooking pot, a straw rain cape. THE ROAD AND THE COUNTRY CARRY THE FRAME: a rutted earth track running away through open country — flat loess fields, bare willows, a low earth wall, a far range of hills, an empty horizon and no destination in sight. Dust raised by the wheels hanging in the light. THE UPPER HALF OF THE FRAME IS OPEN SKY, huge and pale and cold, holding tone everywhere and blown out nowhere.
