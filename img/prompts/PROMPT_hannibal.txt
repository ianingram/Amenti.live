WHY NEW CARTHAGE AND NOT CARTHAGE. Hannibal left Carthage for Spain at NINE — that
departure is The Oath of Enmity, the first work in library/hannibal.json — and did not
see the city again until he was forty-four, returning to fight Zama. There is no
handsome young adult Hannibal in Carthage. New Carthage, Qart Hadasht, is the city the
Barcids built in Spain to be a second Carthage: a great Mediterranean harbour, a palace
on the hill, and the silver mines that poured out three hundred pounds a day and made
the family richer than Rome. He took command there at twenty-six and marched out in
218 at twenty-nine. One substitution — which Punic city — and everything else is true.

THE WEALTH IS IN THE CLOTH AND THE HARBOUR, NOT ON THE MAN. Tyrian purple, which
Carthage made its first fortune selling, and gold at the throat; then silver pigs going
up a plank, warships in ranks, an elephant walking aboard, and a six-storey city behind.
That reads far richer than armour would, and it keeps the plate out of fantasy.

PAIRS AGAINST THE TERMINAL PLATE. That one is the Alps later the same year: the same
man, both eyes still good, purple cloak, in the snow with everything already going
wrong. This is the harbour he left from.

Hannibal Barca at the harbour of New Carthage, on the eve of the expedition. Qart Hadasht in Spain, spring 218 BC. TWENTY-NINE YEARS OLD, HANDSOME, WITH DEEP BROWN SKIN AND AFRICAN FEATURES — a broad nose, full lips, high strong cheekbones, tightly curled black hair cropped close to the skull, clean-shaven or a few days of stubble, both eyes clear and level. Physically light and hard rather than heavy: a young man who sleeps on the ground by choice. Wearing FINE PUNIC CIVILIAN DRESS, and the wealth is in the cloth: a long tunic of very fine bleached linen, and over it a mantle of TYRIAN PURPLE — the deep near-black wine purple that Carthage made its first fortune selling — with a woven gold-thread border, pinned at the shoulder by a heavy gold fibula. A broad gold torc or collar at the throat, gold rings, ivory-and-silver hilted falcata slung at the hip on a tooled leather baldric. Bare-headed. Standing at the head of the great stone quay, one hand resting on a mooring bollard, half turned to the viewer with the harbour open behind him. THE HARBOUR AND THE CITY CARRY THE WEALTH: a deep sheltered basin crowded with shipping — beaked Punic warships drawn up in ordered ranks, round-bellied merchantmen loading, gangplanks down; porters carrying SILVER PIGS FROM THE MINES up a plank in a chain, stacked ingots on the quay, amphorae, coils of rope, crated ivory; a war elephant being walked slowly aboard a broad transport, ears out, handlers at its head. Behind and above the harbour the city rises in terraces: tall stuccoed Punic houses five and six storeys high, painted ochre and white, flat roofs, palms, a great temple platform with an Egyptian cavetto cornice and a Tanit standard, the palace on the hill. KEY LIGHT FROM THE VIEWER'S LEFT: hard low evening sun coming in off the water almost horizontally, blazing on the wet stone of the quay, on the gold at his throat and shoulder, on the bronze rams and the silver on the plank, and lighting the purple mantle through from the edge; every shadow long and hard, and the shadow side of the frame falling to true black. Dust and chaff and gulls in the light. camera at chest height from a few paces along the quay. THREE GUARDS, all default failures. ONE, HE IS A NORTH AFRICAN — render him with AFRICAN FEATURES and DEEP BROWN SKIN: a broad nose, full lips, high strong cheekbones, tightly curled black hair cut close. Libyan and Numidian rather than Levantine. NOT a Roman, NOT a fair European, NOT a Greek hero type, NOT olive, NOT pale, and NOT a swarthy Italian. Carthage was an African city that had stood five hundred years among African peoples and married into them; no likeness of Hannibal survives, so this is a reading of the evidence rather than a portrait, and it is the reading this deck takes. TWO, THIS IS 218 BC AND BOTH EYES ARE STILL GOOD — he loses the sight of the right one to ophthalmia the following year, so no blind eye, no scar, no clouding on this plate. THREE, NO ARMOUR AND NO FANTASY — he is not kitted for battle here and there is no lion skin, no crested helm, no gold filigree breastplate. He is a rich young Carthaginian aristocrat in fine civilian dress, and the wealth is in the cloth, the harbour and the city, not bolted onto him. Visible atmosphere catches the key so the light reads as a physical presence in the air and separates the figure from the background. Cinematic monochrome character plate, near black-and-white with a single warm accent carried only on metal. EVERY SURFACE IS WET, OILED OR POLISHED so it throws a hard specular highlight — nothing matte, nothing that eats the light. Hard highlights run along every fold and edge; deep true blacks in between. One dominant key, hard-edged, with a clear terminator across the face and body; the shadow side falls almost to black and dissolves into the ground, held only by a thin rim of bounce. Full tonal range with small blown speculars. Volume carried by continuous tonal turn. Fine film grain, deep vignette, high micro-contrast. Waist-up, cropped just below the belt. Subject centred, facing the viewer, shoulders filling the width of the frame. The head occupies the top third with a hand's width of space above it. Vertical format, aspect ratio 9:16. Background is a dark, shallow-focus suggestion of place, two stops below the figure. Both hands fully visible and clearly resolved — palm, finger mass, thumb, knuckles, tendons on the back of the hand. THE CROSSING OF THE ALPS, WITH HANNIBAL SMALL IN IT. October, 218 BC — two years before Cannae, so BOTH EYES ARE STILL GOOD. This is a portrait of the mountain first and of the man second. A narrow ledge track cut along the face of an enormous scree-and-rock wall that goes up out of frame and drops away thousands of feet on the other side into blue shadow. Along it, strung out and tiny, the column: infantry in file bent under packs, mules, Numidian horses led not ridden, a supply cart, men hauling on ropes at a bad step — and ONE GAUNT MUD-CAKED ELEPHANT, ribs showing, head down, being coaxed past a narrow place. Fresh snow lying over old grey rock, snow blowing off the ridge in long streamers, the track a churn of trodden slush and mud, ice in the shadowed clefts. Far below and far ahead, through a gap in the cloud, the first green of the Italian plain. HANNIBAL IS ONE SMALL FIGURE off to one side, halted at the edge of the drop with his cloak whipping, looking forward down the pass at what is coming — identifiable only by the dark purple cloak and by standing still while everything else moves. KEY LIGHT FROM THE VIEWER'S RIGHT: hard low winter sun breaking through cloud and striking one wall of the pass and the snow on it so it blazes white, while the opposite wall and the drop below fall two stops down into blue-black. Blown snow and ice crystals catch the sun in the air. camera high and well back on the opposite side of the pass, so the mountain reads before the man does. THREE GUARDS, all of which the model fails by default. ONE, HE IS A NORTH AFRICAN — render him with AFRICAN FEATURES and DEEP BROWN SKIN: a broad nose, full lips, high strong cheekbones, tightly curled black hair cut close. Libyan and Numidian rather than Levantine. NOT a Roman, NOT a fair European, NOT a Greek hero type, NOT olive, NOT pale. TWO, NO FANTASY ARMOUR — no lion skin, no horned or crested fantasy helm, no ornate filigree. He wore what a Hellenistic commander wore and, after Trasimene, captured Roman kit. THREE, ELEPHANTS ARE NOT HIS BADGE — at most one or two, gaunt, mud-caked and miserable; nearly all of them died in the mountains and none of them won him a battle. Visible atmosphere catches the key so the light reads as a physical presence in the air and separates the figure from the background. Cinematic monochrome character plate, near black-and-white with a single warm accent carried only on metal. EVERY SURFACE IS WET, OILED OR POLISHED so it throws a hard specular highlight — nothing matte, nothing that eats the light. Hard highlights run along every fold and edge; deep true blacks in between. One dominant key, hard-edged, with a clear terminator across the face and body; the shadow side falls almost to black and dissolves into the ground, held only by a thin rim of bounce. Full tonal range with small blown speculars. Volume carried by continuous tonal turn. Fine film grain, deep vignette, high micro-contrast. A WIDE MOUNTAIN PORTRAIT. The crossing is the subject and Hannibal is in it — he is SMALL, off-centre, and occupies less than a fifth of the frame height, with the pass, the drop and the strung-out column taking the rest. Nobody looks at the camera. Great empty air above. Vertical format, aspect ratio 9:16. The mountain is IN FOCUS and fully described — this plate is about the crossing. Depth comes from snow-haze and falling light down the length of the pass, not blur. Both hands fully visible and clearly resolved — palm, finger mass, thumb, knuckles, tendons on the back of the hand. Headgear sits firmly ON the skull and casts a shadow onto the brow that binds it to the head. Face weathered and specific, skull structure reading through: brow ridge, cheekbone, jaw, temple. Skin has pores, scars and a faint sheen. He is too far off for expression. He reads as a figure who has stopped to look at what is ahead of him. Photographic realism.
