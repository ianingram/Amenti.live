### PLATO — CARD
status:  SHIPPED — first plate in the deck to measure non-zero head room

status:      AWAITING GENERATION — 9:16, 2K, polish ON. key: viewer's right (torches)

Plato teaching at dusk on the plateau above Athens. The high ground west of the city, about 385 BC. He is about forty: deep bronzed brown skin, black hair in tight curls, a LARGE FULL CURLY BLACK BEARD, and the build of the wrestler he was — wide shoulders, thick neck, deep chest, heavy arms, hard rather than heavy. Bare to the waist, the skin OILED so it throws hard speculars, a plain undyed wool himation across the lap and one shoulder. SEATED AT A LOW STONE PODIUM, one forearm resting on it, WRITING — cutting into a WAX TABLET with a bronze stylus, head down, mid-thought, glancing up at whoever just spoke. Around him a RAKED SAND FLOOR on the open plateau, groomed in long clean grooves, and on it plain stone benches in two short rows where his PUPILS sit: boys and young men in white chitons and himations of undyed linen, ten or twelve of them, each with a WAX TABLET on his knee and a bronze stylus, some writing, some listening, one leaning forward to argue. A YOUNG BOY WITH A WOODEN RAKE works the sand at the near edge, unhurried, not part of the class. TORCHES SET DIRECTLY INTO THE SAND in iron sockets around the floor, four or five of them, already lit and streaming in the evening wind — they are the KEY, warm and low and from the viewer's right, raking across the oiled skin and the white linen and the grooved sand, and the shadow side of every figure falls to true black. BEYOND THEM THE PLATEAU DROPS AWAY and the view opens west: the plain, and past it THE SEA — the Saronic Gulf lying flat and pale, islands low on the water, the last of the sun gone down behind them leaving a deep banded dusk sky, orange low to indigo high, with the first stars. Nothing in the sky is blown out; it holds tone everywhere. Sand dust and torch smoke hanging in the light. camera low, at bench height from among the pupils, looking past him at the sea. FOUR GUARDS. ONE, HE IS A DARK-SKINNED GREEK — deep bronzed brown skin, BLACK hair in tight curls and a LARGE FULL CURLY BLACK BEARD, dark eyes. NOT pale, NOT fair, NOT a white marble statue brought to life. TWO, HE IS BROAD AND HARD — 'Platon' is a nickname meaning BROAD and he wrestled at the Isthmian games. Wide shoulders, thick neck, deep chest, slabbed muscle. HEAVY BUT HARD: no soft belly, no paunch, not fat, and not a frail sage. THREE, NO TOGAS — a toga is Roman. Greeks wear the chiton and the himation, and this is fourth-century Athens. FOUR, THE TABLETS ARE WAX, NOT CLAY — wooden frames filled with dark beeswax, cut with a bronze stylus. Clay tablets are Mesopotamian and a thousand years earlier. Visible atmosphere catches the key so the light reads as a physical presence in the air and separates the figure from the background. Cinematic monochrome character plate, near black-and-white with a single warm accent carried only on metal. EVERY SURFACE IS WET, OILED OR POLISHED so it throws a hard specular highlight — nothing matte, nothing that eats the light. Hard highlights run along every fold and edge; deep true blacks in between. One dominant key, hard-edged, with a clear terminator across the face and body; the shadow side falls almost to black and dissolves into the ground, held only by a thin rim of bounce. Full tonal range with small blown speculars. Volume carried by continuous tonal turn. Fine film grain, deep vignette, high micro-contrast. GIVE IT AIR. Full scene, the figures placed LOW in the frame with the plateau edge, the sea and the sky taking THE UPPER HALF. Plato sits off-centre and does not dominate; the space and the view are part of the picture. Vertical format, aspect ratio 9:16. Background is a dark, shallow-focus suggestion of place, two stops below the figure. Both hands fully visible and clearly resolved — palm, finger mass, thumb, knuckles, tendons on the back of the hand. Headgear sits firmly ON the skull and casts a shadow onto the brow that binds it to the head. Face weathered and specific, skull structure reading through: brow ridge, cheekbone, jaw, temple. Skin has pores, scars and a faint sheen. He is mid-word, head down over the tablet, then glancing up at a pupil. Absorbed in the lesson, not addressing the viewer. Photographic realism. ### PLATO — TERMINAL

THE ALLEGORY OF THE CAVE, WITH PLATO SMALL IN IT. He has come back down on purpose — the Republic says the philosopher must return into the cave — and he is watching. This is a portrait of the cave first and of the man second. A long low rock chamber. Along the near side, PRISONERS SEATED IN A ROW FACING AWAY FROM US toward the far wall, necks and ankles held by bronze rings and chain so they cannot turn; ragged, thin, generations old, unaware of anything behind them. Behind and above them a raised stone WALKWAY like a low parapet, and along it figures moving with objects held up over their heads — carved animals, vessels, human shapes on poles. BEHIND THE WALKWAY, A GREAT FIRE burning on the rock floor, the only real light in the chamber. ON THE FAR WALL IN FRONT OF THE PRISONERS, THE SHADOWS: enormous, black, sharp-edged, thrown many times life size, the whole wall alive with them — and those shadows are the largest thing in the frame. Far off at the back, a narrow passage climbing away with a thin cold shaft of DAYLIGHT falling down it, pale and distant and touching nothing. PLATO IS ONE SMALL FIGURE standing at the foot of that passage in his himation, just at the edge of the firelight, half in shadow, looking down the chamber at the prisoners and the wall. KEY LIGHT IS THE FIRE, from the viewer's left, low and hard and warm; it lights the walkway figures from beneath, throws the shadows, and falls off fast so the roof and the corners of the chamber go to true black. The daylight from the passage is cold, pale, small and secondary and must never compete with it. Smoke and dust in the firelight. camera low and well back on the chamber floor behind the prisoners, so the cave and the shadowed wall read before the man does. FOUR GUARDS. ONE, HE IS A DARK-SKINNED GREEK — deep bronzed olive-brown skin, BLACK hair and a short black beard, dark eyes. Athenian men lived and trained outdoors and their own vase painters drew them dark. NOT pale, NOT fair, NOT blond, NOT a white marble statue brought to life. TWO, HE IS BROAD AND HEAVILY MUSCLED — 'Platon' is a nickname meaning BROAD and he wrestled at the Isthmian games. A wrestler's build: thick neck, wide shoulders, deep chest, heavy thighs. NOT a frail sage, NOT thin, NOT old on this plate. THREE, HE IS ABOUT THIRTY AND HANDSOME — a hard, clear, unlined face. FOUR, NO ANACHRONISM — no Roman toga, no medieval scholar's robe, no book, no scroll-and-lectern. Fourth-century Athens only. Visible atmosphere catches the key so the light reads as a physical presence in the air and separates the figure from the background. Cinematic monochrome character plate, near black-and-white with a single warm accent carried only on metal. EVERY SURFACE IS WET, OILED OR POLISHED so it throws a hard specular highlight — nothing matte, nothing that eats the light. Hard highlights run along every fold and edge; deep true blacks in between. One dominant key, hard-edged, with a clear terminator across the face and body; the shadow side falls almost to black and dissolves into the ground, held only by a thin rim of bounce. Full tonal range with small blown speculars. Volume carried by continuous tonal turn. Fine film grain, deep vignette, high micro-contrast. A WIDE CAVE PORTRAIT. The cave is the subject and Plato is in it — he is SMALL, off to one side, under a fifth of the frame height, with the fire, the walkway, the prisoners and the enormous shadowed wall taking the rest. Nobody looks at the camera. Great air above. Vertical format, aspect ratio 9:16. The cave is IN FOCUS and fully described — this plate is about the cave. Depth comes from firelight falling off into blackness, not from blur. Both hands fully visible and clearly resolved — palm, finger mass, thumb, knuckles, tendons on the back of the hand. Headgear sits firmly ON the skull and casts a shadow onto the brow that binds it to the head. Face weathered and specific, skull structure reading through: brow ridge, cheekbone, jaw, temple. Materials behave like real materials under a hard light. Sharp on the face, softer toward the frame edge.
