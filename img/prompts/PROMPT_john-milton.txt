### JOHN MILTON — CARD

produced:    nothing yet
status:      AWAITING GENERATION — 9:16, 2K, polish ON. key: viewer's right

John Milton at the door of his house in Bunhill Fields, London, about 1665. Fifty-seven, blind for thirteen years. A fine-boned fair face, clean-shaven but for a small neat moustache, light brown hair worn long to the shoulders and parted in the middle, going grey at the temples. Wearing a plain COARSE GREY CLOTH COAT — he was seen sitting at his door in one to take the air — over a plain dark doublet and a plain white linen falling band, plain dark breeches, worn shoes. SEATED IN A PLAIN OAK CHAIR SET OUTSIDE HIS DOOR ON THE STEP, upright, both hands resting on the head of a walking stick planted between his knees. His face is LIFTED AND TURNED SLIGHTLY UP AND PAST THE VIEWER, eyes open, clear and grey-blue, aimed at nothing — he is LISTENING to the street. Around him: a narrow brick London street after rain, wet cobbles, a low iron rail, a bootscraper, ivy on the brick, the door standing half open behind him with dark inside. Coal smoke lying low. Everything wet throwing hard highlights. KEY LIGHT FROM THE VIEWER'S RIGHT: hard low late sun coming down the length of the street between the houses, striking him full in the face and lighting the open eyes brilliantly — the light he cannot see is the brightest thing on him — while the doorway behind and the shadow side fall to true black. camera at chest height from a few paces along the street. FOUR GUARDS, and the first is the whole plate. ONE, HE IS BLIND AND IT DOES NOT SHOW. His eyes are OPEN, CLEAR, GREY-BLUE AND UNBLEMISHED — contemporaries recorded that they looked perfect, that nobody could tell. NOT closed, NOT clouded, NOT white, NOT scarred, NOT squinting, NO blindfold and NO dark glasses. What gives it away is only that they are aimed at nothing and do not track: he faces slightly past whatever is near him. TWO, HE IS NOT AN OLD BEARDED PROPHET. Clean-shaven but for a small neat moustache, light brown hair worn long to the shoulders and parted in the middle, a fine-boned fair face — at Cambridge they called him the Lady of Christ's. THREE, HE IS NEVER READING AND NEVER HOLDING A BOOK. He cannot. Any book in frame is shut, or held by somebody else. FOUR, PLAIN ENGLISH DRESS OF THE 1660s — a plain dark or grey coat, plain linen falling band. No Cavalier lace, no clerical robes, no laurel wreath. Visible atmosphere catches the key so the light reads as a physical presence in the air and separates the figure from the background. Cinematic monochrome character plate, near black-and-white with a single warm accent carried only on metal. EVERY SURFACE IS WET, OILED OR POLISHED so it throws a hard specular highlight — nothing matte, nothing that eats the light. Hard highlights run along every fold and edge; deep true blacks in between. One dominant key, hard-edged, with a clear terminator across the face and body; the shadow side falls almost to black and dissolves into the ground, held only by a thin rim of bounce. Full tonal range with small blown speculars. Volume carried by continuous tonal turn. Fine film grain, deep vignette, high micro-contrast. Waist-up, cropped just below the belt. Subject centred, facing the viewer, shoulders filling the width of the frame. The head occupies the top third with a hand's width of space above it. Vertical format, aspect ratio 9:16. Background is a dark, shallow-focus suggestion of place, two stops below the figure. Both hands fully visible and clearly resolved — palm, finger mass, thumb, knuckles, tendons on the back of the hand. Headgear sits firmly ON the skull and casts a shadow onto the brow that binds it to the head. Face weathered and specific, skull structure reading through: brow ridge, cheekbone, jaw, temple. Skin has pores, scars and a faint sheen. Expression composed and level, mouth closed, eyes catching a single sharp highlight. Photographic realism. Materials behave like real materials under a hard light. Sharp on the face, softer toward the frame edge.

Fifty-five, blind. SEATED SIDEWAYS IN A HIGH-BACKED ARM CHAIR beside a tall casement window, head TIPPED BACK AND TURNED UP TOWARD THE WINDOW, mouth open mid-line, both hands loose on the chair arms, ONE FINGER LIFTED to hold the rhythm. His eyes are open, clear and aimed at nothing. HE IS NOT TOUCHING PAPER. At a small table a little apart, A YOUNG WOMAN IN PLAIN DRESS WRITES AT HIS DICTATION — quill moving, head down, a sheet half filled in a fast hand, an inkpot, a penknife, a candle burnt out, more sheets weighted with a stone. She does not look at him and he cannot look at her. Around them a bare plain room: lime-washed wall, a few shut books stacked on a chest, a lute leaning in the corner, a cold hearth. KEY LIGHT FROM THE VIEWER'S LEFT — hard low first sun coming flat through the casement, laying a bright bar across the floor and up his chest and face and blazing on the open unseeing eyes, catching the wet ink and the quill; the depth of the room falls to true black.
