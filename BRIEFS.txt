TheBook of the Dead

How this machine was built, written against the machine

Budge's Book of the Dead is a collection of roughly
a hundred and ninety spells — each one named, numbered, and invoked to pass a
gate. That is a function signature. This book runs the same apparatus
in every chapter — the Spell, the Mechanism, the Primer, the Plate, the
Roadmap — so one text serves the reader who came for the story, the reader
learning to write code, and the reader who wants the roadmap.
