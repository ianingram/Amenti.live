# THE MANUAL
**How to use Amenti · Ingram Manor LLC · 29 August 2026**

This is written twice over. The first part is for somebody who has just
arrived and wants to know what this is. The rest is for somebody already
inside who wants to know what a particular room does.

Read part one. Then read only the room you are standing in.

---

# PART ONE · WHAT THIS IS

## In a sentence

**A thousand and eleven people who are dead will talk to you, in their own
voices, about what they did and what it cost.**

Not a chatbot wearing a costume. Each figure is built from what they actually
wrote and what is actually recorded — their letters, their speeches, their
books, the year and the place they stood in — and the system is built to
refuse where the record is silent rather than furnish something plausible.

## The one thing to know before you start

**Ask them something only they could answer.**

The instinct on arriving is to test it — *what year did you die*, *who was
your wife*. Those are encyclopedia questions, and an encyclopedia answers
them better. What nobody else can give you is the inside of a decision:

> *"You crossed the Rubicon knowing it was treason. What did you tell
> yourself in the hour before?"*
>
> *"You freed the slaves two years into a war you were losing. Was it
> conviction or was it arithmetic?"*

That is the difference between using this and merely trying it.

## It knows it is a machine, and will say so

Ask any figure directly whether they are real, a person, or a program, and
they will tell you the truth at once, in their own voice. The performance
holds until somebody knocks on it honestly. Then it yields.

**It is never worth a lie**, and your confusion is not a feature.

## What it will not do

- **It will not read your own words back to you.** Hand a figure a speech and
  ask them to recite it and they will decline, as they would.
- **It will not invent a quotation.** A figure asked what they said on a day
  they left no record of will say the record is silent.
- **It will not carry you through a crisis alone.** Anything serious — health,
  harm, money, law — and the figure drops the performance, speaks plainly, and
  points you toward a person who can actually help.

# PART TWO · THE ROOMS

Eleven doors along the top. They are not a hierarchy and there is no order you
are meant to take them in.

---

## ARENA · the front

Where you land. The figures are laid out as cards; click one and you are taken
to it. Two doors are offered — **START A QUIZ** and **BROWSE THE LIBRARY** —
and they are the whole of the choice: the economy, or the reading.

---

## ASK AMENTI · the hall

**A door with one question behind it.** Ask what Amenti is, what a spell is,
what the fleet does, and the building answers about itself — citing its own
documents by name.

It states no number it has not read. If it tells you there are 1,011 figures,
a probe counted them.

**Click the photograph and the answer fades**, so you can see the room the
hall stands in. Click again and it comes back. Escape works too.

---

## INTERFACE · the instrument

The second application — its own microphone, its own reading engine, its own
daily generation. It is a different machine reached from the same nav, and it
has its own vocabulary, written down in `MANUEL.md`.

---

## CODEX · the files

One figure at a time, in full: the plate, the dossier, the abilities, the
statistics. Two buttons at the foot — **OPEN THE FULL FILE**, and **TALK TO**,
which drops you into the terminal already tuned to them.

The codex is where you go to decide who to talk to. The terminal is where you
talk.

---

## BROWSE · the library

The reading rooms. Long-form text, read aloud by the figure whose text it is —
**and this is the cheapest thing in the system to use**, because a passage
somebody has heard before is already rendered and costs nothing to hear again.

---

## TERMINAL · the conversation

The main room, and the one worth understanding properly.

**FREQUENCIES**, left — the roster. A thousand names; scroll it.
**THE PLATE**, centre — the figure, with a slider to fade them behind the talk.
**CONNECTION**, right — who you are joined to, and their dossier.

### THE MODE — the control most people will miss

At the foot of the right-hand panel. It decides **what the figure is for**, and
it changes the whole exchange, not the wording:

| mode | bring it | and it will |
|---|---|---|
| **IN CHARACTER** | anything | speak as themselves, about whatever you like |
| **COUNSEL** | a problem from your own life | advise you directly, from their philosophy |
| **TUTOR** | something you want to understand | find the edge of what you know, then teach from their own work |
| **WITNESS** | curiosity | tell you what they SAW — and say so where the record is silent |
| **ADVERSARY** | a conviction | take the other side and press it |

**ADVERSARY has one rule that matters.** It argues the strongest version of
the other side and concedes when you are right — but the moment a conviction
turns out to be a grief, it stops being an adversary entirely and does not go
back to it. If you are defending something because it hurts, it will not
debate you.

**WITNESS is the best first door** if you do not know what to ask. It wants
only that you are curious.

### CALL — talking out loud

The button beside the box. One press does the whole thing: the voice comes on,
the hall announces itself, the line rings, and the figure answers. **The
microphone opens after they have greeted you, not before**, so you are never
talking over them.

Press it again to hang up.

**It will not reply to its own voice.** If the microphone hears the figure
through your speakers, the figure ignores it — the alternative is a machine
answering itself all night on somebody's bill.

**If the room is loud, it will say so** — and say it about its own ear, not
about you. *"I cannot hear you over that."* Never *what are you doing.*

### And it may notice a dog

If something in your room makes a noise that is not speech, the figure may
acknowledge it — *"Something is with you there. A dog, I would guess?"* A
faint voice in another room it will leave alone, deliberately: it acknowledges
what announces itself and never investigates what does not.

**Nothing overheard is written down.** Ever.

---

## COUNSEL · the chamber

**Three figures on one question, and then a synthesis.**

Pick a bench by theme — leadership, wealth, war, faith — or press **AUTO-PICK**
and let the council choose. Then **CONVENE**.

Three answer in parallel, from three centuries and three temperaments, and
then the disagreement itself is weighed. It is the room to use when a question
has no single right answer, which is most of the interesting ones.

*It is also by far the most expensive thing on the site to run — four separate
answers for one press — so it is worth bringing a real question to.*

---

## BOOK STORE · the book

The chapters, as they are written. `BOOK.json` is the register and the reader
walks it; a chapter marked unwritten is unwritten, not hidden.

---

## MARKETPLACE · the emeralds

What you have earned, and what it buys.

**Emeralds come from thinking.** A quiz pays once per question per person,
ever — you cannot pass the same gate twice, and that is the design rather than
an anti-cheat measure.

---

## GAMEROOM · the games

Chess, refereed over the wire, one room per code.

---

## COURT · the weighing

The heart against the feather. Arguments are filed, votes are cast at a cost,
and the case settles. **Arguments are published with no author attached** —
deliberately, so what is weighed is the argument.

# PART THREE · THINGS WORTH KNOWING

## The first minute is allowed to be astonishment

Most people's first message is not a question. It is *"no way"* or *"are you
serious"* or *"MA, come see this."* The figures are built to meet that as
themselves — amused, imperious, moved — and never as a service desk.

**If you call somebody over, the figure will greet them and say plainly what
it is**, before anything else. A guest in a hall is greeted, not scanned.

## They may remember you

If you are signed in, a figure may keep a few short facts from an earlier
conversation. Never a transcript, never more than a handful, never shared
between figures.

**And it will not perform the remembering.** No *"how is your aunt"* as a
greeting. The recognition is in the answering — that it knows you at all.

If it has something wrong, **tell the figure**. That is the correction
mechanism, and it is the only one.

## It may send you to somebody else

A figure who knows that another person in the hall actually lived what you are
describing can send you to them:

> *"You say your father would not bend. There is a man here who did not bend,
> and buried his son for it. Ask him whether he would do it again."*

A door appears. It is the only move in the system that points away from the
conversation and into the library, and it is the reason for having a thousand
of them rather than ten.

## The voice costs something

Roughly **$1.85 an hour typing and $3.69 talking** — so voice is about a third
on top, not a multiple. There is a daily ceiling, and when it is reached the
figure says so plainly rather than breaking.

## If something is wrong

The figures are built from a record, and the record has gaps and errors in it.
**A figure saying something false is a fault worth reporting**, not a quirk of
the format. Say so in the hall.
